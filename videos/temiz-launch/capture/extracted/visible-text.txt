Temiz — Turkish for clean. A free, open-source Mac maintenance app by Berk Boz (brk.bz).
One button Check up: looks for junk, threats, maintenance that is due and duplicates, all at once. Nothing changes until you review the results and press Run.
Clean: caches, logs, developer leftovers and old installers; everything goes to the Trash first.
Protect: flags cracked or tampered apps, suspicious background services and open ports.
Speed: what starts with your Mac, and maintenance tasks.
Apps: removes apps with their leftovers; checks for updates when you ask.
Clutter: large, old and duplicate files. Space: a map of your disk.
Monitor: CPU, memory, battery, disk and network, also from the menu bar.
No helper running as root. No account, no telemetry. No network unless you click.
Free & open source. brk.bz/temiz
