## Intent

Brand / launch film for Messy, a free, open-source Mac
maintenance app by Berk Boz (brk.bz). The broom mascot enters, starts the
Check up ring, the result cards fill in, the broom sweeps dust clouds away, and
it ends on the logo and "Free & open source". Calm and understated like brk.bz,
but warm and charming because of the mascot. Promo intent (sell), built from the
real app screens. No voice-over.

## Notes

- Palette from the icon: mint/teal sky (#0E9F9A, #1FBF73, mint #54D6C2) for clean, coral #FF856B for mess, dark ink background like the site. Wordmark: lowercase 'messy' with a hand-drawn coral squiggle under it.
- Never show the user's personal app list or file paths beyond what the screenshots already crop.
- Wording on screen: plain, sentence case, no exclamation marks.
